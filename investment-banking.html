Syndicate Architecture

The Consortium Syndication Model

How Omni Fincon anchors multi-lender consortia to fund massive corporate and infrastructure outlays.

Phase 1: Deal Modeling & IM

We formulate the Information Memorandum (IM), historical EBITDA restatements, financial covenants, and credit rating optimization reports.

Phase 2: Lead Bank Underwriting

Omni Fincon secures the Lead Arranger sanction letter with anchor underwriting commitments, establishing baseline interest margins and security terms.

Phase 3: Syndication Sell-Down

Participating consortium banks subscribe to down-sold tranches under a unified Inter-Creditor Agreement (ICA), closing the entire financing round.
