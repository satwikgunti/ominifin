Capital Practices

Industrial, Infrastructure & Enterprise Debt

We represent operating corporations, concessionaires, and industrial promoters before bank credit committees, institutional rating boards, and consortium lead arrangers.

Infrastructure & EPC Concessions

Project term debt and non-fund facilities for HAM, BOT, and EPC infrastructure projects including expressways, bridges, ports, and urban water transmission utilities. We structure Bank Guarantees (BG), Mobilization Advance limits, and performance lines.

Tickets: ₹100 Cr – ₹1,500 Cr+ • HAM / BOT / EPC Structures

Industrial Manufacturing & Capex

Long-term debt for greenfield manufacturing units and brownfield capacity expansions in integrated steel, cement, auto components, precision engineering, textiles, and chemicals. Tailored to plant gestation and cash ramp-up curves.

Tickets: ₹50 Cr – ₹600 Cr+ • Plant & Machinery Term Debt

Power & Renewable Energy Utilities

Specialized project finance for utility-scale solar parks, wind power farms, clean energy transmission grids, and Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS). Structured around long-term Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs) with central and state utilities.

Tenors: 12 – 18 Years • PPA-Backed Project Finance

Healthcare, Hospitals & Life Sciences

Capital structuring for multi-speciality tertiary hospitals, medical college campuses, diagnostic chain expansions, and API pharma manufacturing facilities. Tailored debt structures factoring in accreditation and operational gestation.

Tickets: ₹40 Cr – ₹450 Cr • Medical Equipment & Project Term Debt

Multi-Modal Logistics & Cold Storage

Financing Multi-Modal Logistics Parks (MMLPs), Inland Container Depots (ICD), automated fulfillment centers, and temperature-controlled cold chain networks. Combining asset-backed term loans with long-term 3PL tenancy discounting.

Tickets: ₹50 Cr – ₹350 Cr • Supply Chain Asset Finance

Corporate Debt Consortia & Working Capital

Lead arranger for multi-bank consortium loans and sole-bank working capital facilities. We structure Cash Credit / Overdraft (CC/OD), Letters of Credit (LC), Bank Guarantees (BG), and External Commercial Borrowings (ECB) under unified ICAs.

Consortium Leadership • Inter-Creditor Agreement (ICA) Desk

Balance Sheet Restructuring & Debt Takeover

Refinancing high-cost historical borrowings, takeout financing with Tier-1 PSU and private banks, covenant renegotiation, and credit rating optimization. Achieving meaningful cost of capital reduction (150 – 350 bps) and tenor elongation.

Cost of Capital Reduction • DSCR Enhancement

Stressed Asset Resolution & Bank OTS

Structuring One-Time Settlements (OTS) with banking consortia, pre-IBC resolution capital, bridge debt, and operating asset carve-outs. We lead formal financial negotiations with lender consortiums to preserve enterprise going-concern value.

Bank Consortium Negotiations • Resolution Debt Syndication

Techno-Economic Viability (TEV) & DPRs

Formulation of Detailed Project Reports (DPR), Techno-Economic Viability (TEV) studies mandated by bank credit committees, financial sensitivity modeling, and Independent Engineering Review (LIE) coordination for project sanctions.

Bankable TEV Models • Credit Committee Compliance
Consortium Execution

The Multi-Lender Syndication Roadmap

How Omni Fincon anchors multi-bank consortia to finance massive corporate, industrial, and infrastructure outlays.

Phase 1: DPR Formulation & TEV

We formulate the Detailed Project Report (DPR), Techno-Economic Viability (TEV) model, historical EBITDA restatements, financial covenants, and debt service coverage ratios.

Phase 2: Anchor Lead Bank Underwriting

Omni Fincon secures the Lead Arranger sanction letter with anchor underwriting commitments, establishing baseline interest margins, security packages, and trust and retention accounts (TRA).

Phase 3: Consortium Sell-Down & ICA

Participating consortium banks subscribe to down-sold tranches under a unified Inter-Creditor Agreement (ICA), closing the entire financing round with complete disbursement alignment.
