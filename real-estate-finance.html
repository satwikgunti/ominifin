Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to critical questions from real estate promoters regarding sanctions and collateral.

What documents are required for preliminary sanction evaluation?
For initial term sheet modeling, our desk requires: (1) Land title search report & sanction orders, (2) Detailed architect project report detailing built-up area (BUA) and saleable area, (3) RERA registration copy (or sanction timeline), and (4) Developer entity 3-year audited financials with existing bank exposure statements.
Can Omni Fincon arrange funding before RERA approval is obtained?
Yes. Through our specialized Category II Alternative Investment Funds (AIF) and private NBFC partners, we structure pre-RERA land advances and approval debt facilities that convert into standard senior construction finance once final statutory sanctions are issued.
How does Lease Rental Discounting (LRD) work for commercial buildings?
LRD allows building owners to borrow against future contracted rent payments from verified corporate tenants (e.g. IT firms, MNCs, retail anchors). The lender discounts future lease receipts over 9 to 15 years, requiring an escrow mechanism where tenant rents are credited directly to the lending institution.
Can you assist in refinancing an existing high-cost NBFC loan?
Absolutely. High-cost debt takeover and balance sheet refinancing is a primary specialty. When your project hits construction milestones and sales velocity increases, we bring in tier-1 private or public sector banks to refinance existing NBFC debt at rates 200–350 bps lower, dramatically reducing interest burden.
